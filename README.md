# 🛍️ Seasonal Sales Trend Analysis

This project analyzes retail sales data to understand **seasonal sales trends, high and low sales periods, and product category performance**.

The analysis uses Excel to find useful business insights and provide recommendations for **inventory planning, promotions, and staffing**.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

The company was seeing changes in sales but did not clearly understand why.

Management wanted to know:

* Which months and quarters have the highest and lowest sales?
* Are there any seasonal sales patterns?
* Which product categories perform better?
* When should the company increase inventory?
* When should promotions be planned?
* When should staffing be increased or reduced?

Understanding these patterns can help the company plan better and avoid **stock shortages, excess inventory, and poorly timed promotions**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Identify seasonal sales patterns
* Find high and low sales periods
* Analyze product category performance
* Understand monthly and quarterly sales trends
* Support better inventory planning
* Suggest suitable timings for promotions and staffing

---

## 🔍 Analysis Approach

### 1. Seasonality Analysis

Identified monthly and quarterly sales patterns to understand changes in demand.

### 2. Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales over time to understand whether sales were increasing, decreasing, or remaining stable.

### 3. Category Analysis

Compared product categories to identify high- and low-performing categories.

### 4. Monthly and Quarterly Analysis

Analyzed revenue and quantity sold by month and quarter to identify peak and low-demand periods.

### 5. Dashboard Creation

Created an interactive Excel dashboard using PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, and KPI cards.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **1,000 retail transactions**.

| Column           | Description                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date             | Date of the transaction                                    |
| Customer ID      | Unique customer ID                                         |
| Product Category | Product category such as Beauty, Clothing, and Electronics |
| Quantity         | Number of units sold                                       |
| Price per Unit   | Price of one unit                                          |
| Total Amount     | Total revenue from the transaction                         |
| Month            | Month derived from the transaction date                    |
| Quarter          | Quarter derived from the transaction date                  |

---

## 📈 Dashboard

The Excel dashboard includes:

* Total Revenue
* Total Units Sold
* Total Customers
* Average Revenue per Customer
* Average Selling Price
* Revenue by Month
* Revenue by Quarter
* Revenue by Product Category
* Quantity Sold by Product Category
* Monthly and quarterly trends
* Interactive slicers for category, month, and quarter

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Peak and low sales periods
* Seasonal changes in sales
* High- and low-performing product categories
* Changes in customer demand over time
* Periods where inventory and promotions may need more attention

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the company can:

1. Increase inventory before high-demand periods.
2. Plan promotions before peak sales periods.
3. Reduce excess stock during low-demand periods.
4. Plan additional staff during busy periods.
5. Use historical sales patterns to support future planning.
